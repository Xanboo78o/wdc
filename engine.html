what is in the loop

Same idea all three ways — a loop played at a rate. The difference is how long the loop is, and that is everything. Speeding a sample up moves its TIMBRE, not just its pitch: a drum body at 210 Hz played 37× faster to reach an F1's firing rate lands at 7,900 Hz, so the thump becomes hiss. One firing cycle is already the right length, so it is stretched barely an octave and keeps its character. Switch between them at the same RPM and it is obvious.

Throttle does NOT change the pitch — that is the whole point. It opens a filter and lifts the level, so the engine gets harder and brighter without getting higher. Pull it to 0 at a high speed and you should hear an engine on the overrun rather than an engine slowing down.
