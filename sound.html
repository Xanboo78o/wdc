Adam, with the speaker taped under the seat: “it shakes a lil and muffles it like its coming from the engine.” That muffling was an accident — a small driver pointed at the floor through a Bluetooth codec. This makes it deliberate: a lowpass on the ROOM bus only, so the speaker is a thing you feel through the chassis rather than a speaker playing an engine. Wind it down until it stops sounding like a speaker.
Bluetooth is 150–250 ms late. That is fatal for anything with a MOMENT — a kerb strike, a lockup — and completely unnoticeable on continuous sound, which has no moment to be late for. So ROOM gets drone, CANS gets edges.

A tyre is silent until it is near its limit, then rises fast — that threshold is the whole feel of it. Too low and the car squeals in a straight line; too high and the slide has already happened by the time you hear it. That number is what I most need you to judge.

ROAD & WIND — driven by SPEED, not by revs

Adam: “make gear 5 feel like gear 3” — and he is right, because the engine note is rpm / 11000 and nothing else. At the limiter in third and in fifth the revs are identical, so the sound is identical. To the audio, gear 5 is gear 3. What is missing is everything that scales with SPEED: at 208 km/h you should be drowning in roar that is not there at 146.

This layer is a placeholder — it is the tyre sample with the top end taken off, standing in for road roar until a proper CC0 one is sourced. Judge the IDEA, not the sound.
